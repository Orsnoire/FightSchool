## Released behavior

Solo creators intentionally leaving an active fight now complete through the same transition and EXP formula as both teacher end controls. Correct answers, actual damage, blocked damage and actual healing contribute, with the existing incorrect-answer deduction, early-ending proration, stamina and exactly-once persistence. Early exit grants no victory gold or loot. A solo creator leaving ends the room for any joined guildmates; non-host departures and recoverable disconnects retain their existing behavior.

Hex deals MAT + INT initially and MAT + INT − 1 on each subsequent round, applying MDF to each hit. Its full duration follows the casting round, and refreshing cannot cancel an already-due tick. Previews and execution share values. Hemorrhage has its own two-round physical duration. The current player catalog has direct healing but no HoT ability. Existing saved effects retain their persisted values and compatibility semantics; new casts use corrected rules. [Complete rules and investigation](../combat/solo-exit-xp-and-periodic-effects.md).
