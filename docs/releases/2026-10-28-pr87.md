## Released behavior

The teacher guild Members tab shows each player ID, all 12 permanent job levels (0 without progression), lifetime AA earned including spent points, active game hours over the preceding 168 hours, and question-weighted guild accuracy over the preceding 24 hours. Refresh, missing history, partial history, stale/error and empty states are explicit. [Metric definitions and authorization](../TEACHER_GUILD_ROSTER.md).

Activity sampling runs across authenticated student pages, including menus and fights. Hidden tabs, idle time, timer suspension and offline replay are excluded. Server-owned 30-second buckets and pulse receipts prevent duplicate credit from retries and concurrent tabs/devices. First visits establish tracking without awarding time. Historical active hours cannot be reconstructed; each player shows partial history until seven days have elapsed.

Accuracy includes recent question attempts in active fights. Incorrect and recorded unanswered questions count in the denominator. Legacy completed fights without per-question records fall back to stored totals with a visible caveat; records are never counted twice. No observations do not become 0%.
